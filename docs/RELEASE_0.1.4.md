# Metagene 0.1.4

Metagene 0.1.4 upgrades structural evolution to catalog model v3 and revision `modes-colored-mana-v3`.

- Face-aware initial modes distinguish transforming, modal DFC, split, and adventure cards.
- Colored, generic, colorless, hybrid, Phyrexian, and variable costs are parsed into compact profiles.
- Mana trials choose land modes, honor tapped/source timing, pay colored requirements first, and delay cast-dependent rocks or activation-cost fixing.
- Mandatory mana repair follows initialization and mutation. Severe failures cannot enter the frontier or game-validation budget.
- Constructed copy selection is role-aware; Commander and Brawl remain singleton. Per-card Min/Max remains authoritative.
- Candidate inspection shows dedicated/modal lands, early/late W/U/B/R/G/C sources, pip demand, castability, blocking reasons, synergy warnings, and copy rationale.
- Catalog v3 rebuilds automatically. Existing user data is retained; legacy structural results remain inspectable with a re-evaluation warning.
